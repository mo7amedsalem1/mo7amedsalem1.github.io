01 — ABOUT ME

Most security failures don’t start with hackers.
They start with how the system was built.

My professional journey is shaped by one guiding belief: to truly secure a product, you must understand how it generates value, how it scales, and where its logic can fail under pressure.

I began with a strong academic foundation and a multidisciplinary curiosity spanning technology, business, and applied sciences. When I pivoted toward Computer Science with a specialization in Cybersecurity, I carried that curiosity with me — not as indecision, but as perspective.

Parallel to my studies, I worked in the E-commerce sector, contributing to brand development and industrial scaling. That experience taught me something most security discussions ignore: downtime is not a technical issue — it’s a revenue event. Every vulnerability has a business cost.

When I moved deeper into Penetration Testing, I reached a critical realization: exploitation without architectural understanding is reactive. Defense without development insight is fragile.

So I rebuilt my trajectory intentionally.

I immersed myself in backend development and the Software Development Life Cycle, adopting a developer mindset alongside a security one. Today, I focus on securing systems at their core — at the source code, API logic, and business workflows where real risk is born.

Penetration Testing
