HOW I WORK

A clear process.
No mystery report.

The goal is not to produce the biggest list of findings. It is to establish what is exploitable, why it matters, and how to fix it.

01

Scope & understand

Agree on targets, test accounts, permitted techniques, exclusions, and success criteria.

ALIGN
02

Test & validate

Map the attack surface, test key controls manually, and safely verify realistic attack paths.

VERIFY
03

Report & prioritize

Document reproduction steps, evidence, impact, severity rationale, and practical remediation.

EXPLAIN
04

Review & retest

Where agreed, retest fixes and confirm whether the reported issue has been resolved.
